Hi, I am a data researcher and hacker. My interests focus on distributed data processing tools -- mostly targeting maps, robotics, and telematics. I am the author of the Turf geospatial analysis library, as well as a number of other low level geometry and statistics algorithm libraries commonly used by journalists, scientists, researchers, and cartographers. I work at SpaceX, responsible for simulation, telemetry and control planning optimization for the Starlink satellite megaconstellation. In my spare time, I enjoy gardening and music.
